How It Works ⚕

MedRule implements the classic 5-part MYCIN Expert System architecture (Unit IV) without black-box neural networks or external LLM APIs.

Unit II

Knowledge Representation & Text Feature Extraction

Uses propositional IF-THEN production rules for medical knowledge representation, paired with synonym dictionaries, stopword removal, and TF-IDF cosine similarity (in Python) to normalize patient input.

Unit III

Decision Making in Uncertainty (MYCIN Certainty Factors)

Handles overlapping symptoms and uncertain observations using Shortliffe & Buchanan’s Certainty Factor algebra rather than rigid Boolean logic:

Contribution = CF(rule) × min(CF(premise₁), CF(premise₂), ...)
CF_combined(A, B) = A + B × (1 − A)

Unit IV

Real-World Applications & MYCIN Case Study

Directly mirrors the Stanford MYCIN medical consultation system: separating domain rules from the inference engine, triaging urgent red flags, and answering "Why did you reach this diagnosis?"
